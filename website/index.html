Get started

From download to your own setup.

Bald Launcher is a Windows desktop application. Download the installer, open it on your PC, and follow the setup flow in the app.

  • ✓Download the latest installer from GitHub Releases.
  • ✓Install and open Bald Launcher on Windows.
  • ✓Set up your profiles and explore the available tools.

The browser preview is for exploring the interface only. It does not launch Minecraft or access files on your computer.
